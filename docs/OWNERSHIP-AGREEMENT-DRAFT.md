## 2. Shared project ownership

The five named members identify SAQR as a project they are developing together and intend to own jointly. No ownership percentages or unequal shares are specified in this draft. The team does not need to assign percentages for the current academic presentation stage. If a commercial deal, transfer, licensing arrangement, or ownership dispute arises later, the members should document the agreed allocation and obtain appropriate legal advice.

Joint project ownership does not erase each member's authorship of their individual contributions or automatically transfer separately created pre-existing materials.

## 3. Materials covered

Subject to the ownership of third parties and each member's pre-existing materials, the team intends this agreement to cover original project materials created for SAQR, including:

- Original source code and scripts.
- Original architecture, designs, diagrams, and workflows.
- Original documentation, reports, and written materials.
- Original project branding, graphics, and interface designs.
- Original test cases, configurations, and project-specific assets.

## 4. Decisions requiring written unanimous approval

Unless all five members later agree otherwise in a signed written amendment, the following actions require prior written approval from all five members:

- Publishing the repository or project materials publicly.
- Applying or changing a software license.
- Allowing third parties to copy, modify, redistribute, or commercially use the project.
- Selling, assigning, transferring, or exclusively licensing project rights.
- Removing or materially changing this ownership notice.
- Making a public release or distributing a packaged copy of the project.

Approval should be recorded in a durable form, such as a signed document or an email/message from each member that clearly identifies the approved action.

## 5. Individual contributions and pre-existing materials

Each member should document their contributions in the project records. Materials created before joining SAQR, and third-party libraries, tools, datasets, fonts, icons, or other assets, are not automatically transferred to the team by this agreement.

Each member must identify any pre-existing or third-party material they add and record its applicable license or permission.

## 6. Repository access and security

The team intends the GitHub repository to remain private. The repository owner should restrict access to the five approved members, enable two-factor authentication, avoid committing secrets, and review access periodically.

A private GitHub repository is a technical access control. It is not a substitute for this written agreement or other legal protection.

## 7. University and supervisor

The members state that they are not aware of a separate university ownership agreement at the date of signing. Before signing, the team should check the university's graduation-project rules and any forms signed during registration, supervision, or use of university resources.

If a university rule or signed agreement applies, the members must review it and amend this document as needed.

## 8. Disagreements and amendments

The members will first attempt to resolve disagreements through a documented team discussion. Any amendment to this agreement must be written and signed by all five members. This draft does not specify governing law, jurisdiction, remedies, or dispute procedure; obtain local legal advice before final execution.

## 9. Sign-off

By signing below, each member confirms that they have read and understood the completed agreement and voluntarily accept its terms.

Signatures are not required for the current supervisor-presentation stage. This file is an internal draft record of the team's stated intention, not proof of a signed or legally finalized agreement. If the team later needs a formal agreement for commercialization, licensing, transfer, or dispute prevention, all five members should review and sign a completed version, with legal advice where appropriate.
